# Niche Decision — Clover & Bruno Stories
**Status**: LOCKED FINAL 2026-10-29
**Channel**: Clover & Bruno Stories @cloverandbruno
**Replaces**: previous prototype old tribunal concept — ABANDONED

## Final selection

**Photoreal anthropomorphic cat and dog family neighborhood stories**, delivered as warm, witty, complete slice-of-life episodes on Paw Street. The repeatable show is **Paw Street Stories**; the audience watches polite kitten Clover always with Mama Rosa + Papa Fin meet 70-year-old retired grumpy dog Bruno who never says yes first time, mostly with Pip.

**One-sentence value proposition:** Clover & Bruno turns everyday chores, wishes, and small money troubles into cozy cat and dog family stories where polite meets grumpy — and grumpy helps anyway.

## Market gap

Existing supply clusters into: cartoon cat family, photoreal cat-only chore, distress rescue bait, grotesque cat soap opera (buff cat cheating). Opening is **premium repeatable cat and dog family with locked characters and grumpy-to-soft gag**:

- recognizable polite request vs grumpy No in first 45 seconds
- consistent faces (Clover orange onesie, Bruno 70y gray + burnt-orange cardigan and glasses-on-a-chain) = defensible against generic template
- chore prop visible by 0:10 (bike, bag, lawnmower)
- 70% Duo both families = cat n dog channel instantly readable in thumbnail
- warm resolved payoff (help done, shared snack, hug) not distress

## Risk decision

The model must never be the product promise. Viewers should remember Clover's politeness and Bruno's grumpy-then-soft heart, not the generator. Unified photoreal engine for all 7 characters — the old per-character engine split is ABANDONED. LTX-2.5 image-to-video is locked for all 7, see CHARACTER_BIBLE.md.

YouTube July 15 2025 inauthentic content update must be respected: avoid generic repetitive template, off-putting distress, AI personas. Our defense is locked characters + new chore each time + human QC + evidence pack.

Until reference packs for CAST-001 to CAST-007 are approved with portrait + full-body + six-panel + voice, public release may not proceed.
