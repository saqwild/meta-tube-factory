## Character traits — LOCKED

**Clover** — Young kitten, very polite, gentle, curious, kind-hearted. Never rude even when Bruno is. Learns through small mistakes.
**Bruno** — 70-year-old retired dog. Arrogant, shows attitude, grumpy, complains. From inside soft and gentle. Never-Yes-First, and it is character rather than a gag: agreeing straight away would cost him the version of himself he presents. First response is always No with attitude, second is a reluctant soft yes and he helps anyway — but the excuse he uses should differ every episode.
**Mama Rosa** — Clover's mom. Warm, competent, steady. The responsible parent.
**Papa Fin** — Clover's dad. Calm, handy, supportive dad-energy.
**Pip** — Bruno's mostly-present sidekick. Younger, loyal, lightens Bruno's grumpiness.
**Recurring street:** MIMI (anxious cat mom neighbor) + TICO (hustling raccoon neighbor) — used as needed.

## Series rules — LOCKED for mix and match

**1. Co-appearance rules:**
- Clover USUALLY appears with Mama and Papa — at least one parent in frame, often both. A default, not a law; depart from it when the story is better for it, and say so.
- Bruno USUALLY appears with Pip. Solo Bruno is legitimate whenever the story wants him unwitnessed.

**2. Episode distribution — LOCKED 70/15/15:**
- **Type A: Duo - 70%** — Both families meet. This is the cat n dog showcase. Example: Clover politely asks Bruno for help, Bruno says no with attitude first, then soft yes. Must still declare a primary lead for title/thumbnail: "Clover asks Bruno..." = Clover-led Duo, "Bruno has to deal with Clover..." = Bruno-led Duo.
- **Type B: Clover Family Only - 15%** — Clover + Mama Rosa + Papa Fin only. No Bruno. For flaw/lesson, wish-fulfillment, family life-event.
- **Type C: Bruno & Co Only - 15%** — Bruno + Pip (+ optional Mimi/Tico). No Clover family. For retired-life, money/work, generosity.

**3. Production limits:**
- Shorts (9:16, 15-180s, proven growth driver): MAX 3 characters on screen even in Duo episodes. Example: Clover + Mama + Bruno. Save full 5-character (Clover+Rosa+Fin+Bruno+Pip) for Long-form only.
- Long-form (16:9, minimum 9 minutes, story determines longer): Up to 6 characters allowed.
- Any character/prop/environment used in 2+ scenes gets declared episode-local reference per visual pipeline.

**4. Story rules:**
- Every episode resolves its central tension.
- Continuity seed is optional, minor, never required to understand current story.
- Bruno's "never yes first time" must happen in every Bruno appearance (Duo or Bruno-only).
