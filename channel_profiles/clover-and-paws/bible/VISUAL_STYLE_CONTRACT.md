## North star — cozy photoreal family

**Photoreal anthropomorphic cozy family, warm daylight, shallow depth of field:** emotional credibility of a family sitcom, authored texture of soft fur, graphic clarity of hero prop, and restrained warm home edge. Visual system must hide model weaknesses through deliberate framing and warm light, not through dark navy effects.

This is photoreal realism lock: **not painterly graphic-novel realism**, not cel-shaded cartoon, not comic caricature, not flat vector, not glossy 3D/toy rendering, not dark tribunal ink-navy.

Old palette ABANDONED: Ink navy #07131C, Slate #24333D, Aged brass #C79A45, Paper #E8E0D1, Oxblood #7B2D26, Legacy red #A93832 — all ABANDONED.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Display, short overlay (BRUNO SAYS NO) | Fredoka / Rounded sans | Warm cream or white with soft shadow, 2-4 words max |
| Amounts, timestamps, shopping list | Inter / clean sans | Soft wood or Bruno cardigan color, tabular figures |
| Captions | Human-reviewed UTF-8 SRT | Matches dialogue, not generated type |

Generated type is never final delivery type. Add all readable wording (shopping list, market sign) in post.

## World sets — Paw Street expands beyond house

1. **Clover Family Kitchen:** warm cream walls, wood floor, soft daylight, dishes, cheese pizza ingredients, coin jar — recurring visual home for Clover Only and Duo Cozy
2. **Bruno Porch:** soft wood porch, old chair, cane, oil can, newspaper, warm afternoon light — recurring for Bruno & Co and Duo No beat
3. **Paw Street / Market Square:** outdoor market, bakery corner, stalls, shopping bags — for Rescue Dash cart rolling, Road & Muscle crates
4. **Garage:** Papa Fin's garage, tools, truck, oil cans, heavy fridge — for Road & Muscle breakdown and Spy Agency footprints
5. **Old Oak / Bridge / Park / Lake:** outdoor adventure sets — old oak tree creaking, bridge with loose plank, lake picnic, forest edge — show places around town, not just in-house
6. **Spy Nook:** Bruno's detective corner on porch, magnifier, old hat, map — comedic, daytime, warm

Each set must be bright warm daylight, not dark navy. At least one practical light source (window, porch sun) must motivate every scene.

## Image rules — photoreal cozy

- 16:9 master Longs, 9:16 Shorts — compose safe for both
- Character faces and paws receive priority over background detail — fur must pass close-up
- Use shallow-to-moderate depth of field; critical hero prop (bike pump, bag, oil can, magnifier) remains readable
- Practical light: window daylight, porch sun, market sun — warm, not cold fluorescent
- Generated visible text prohibited beyond short number or single word; insert shopping lists, market signs in post
- No real corporate logos, recognizable products, flags as accusation shorthand, or visual copying of living artist
- No distress bait: no syringe, no chased animals, no axe attack, no buff human-bodied cat — all ABANDONED as off-putting
- Outdoor sets must show place: include background hint of Paw Street (houses, market, trees) to avoid generic backdrop
- Max characters: Shorts 2-3 even Duo, Longs up to 6 — prevents AI slop crowd

## Thumbnail contract — cat & dog family

- Use bright warm daylight plate, not darkened tribunal navy
- For Duo 70%, place BOTH cat and dog faces: Clover left 40% polite gentle eyes + Bruno right 40% grumpy scoff + small object center (tire, bag, pump, oil can, magnifier)
- For Clover Only: Clover + Rosa + Fin warm
- For Bruno Only: Bruno + Pip porch
- Place 2-4 words overlay exact line from episode: BRUNO SAYS NO / PLEASE BRUNO? / FIN FALLS / BRUNO SAVES PIP / SPY BRUNO / TRUCK BREAKS — Fredoka rounded, warm cream/white with soft shadow
- Place zero to one small Paw Street silhouette if needed, not brass hairline
- Keep critical content inside central 80% and verify at 10% mobile size — faces must read on phone
- Reserve lower-right 25% width × 18% height for YouTube duration overlay
- Ban arrows, circles, red rings, shocked-face-plus-explosion, more than 4 words, fake news lower-thirds
- Generate 1280×720 16:9 Longs + 1080×1920 9:16 Shorts master; compress below 2 MB
- For Stand Tall arc: Fin small fallen left, Bruno huge standing right, Clover worried middle — text BRUNO STANDS TALL
- For Rescue Dash: Bruno mid-jump, tree/cart, small character below — text BRUNO SAVES PIP
- For Road & Muscle: truck hood up, Fin muscle left, Bruno oil can right, Clover wrench middle
- For Spy Agency: Bruno detective hat left grumpy, Clover magnifier right polite

## Shot-color grammar — cozy + action

- Cozy Home: warm cream / soft wood, stable camera, shallow depth
- Stand Tall: warm but slightly higher contrast when Fin falls, then warm returns when Bruno shoulder-to-shoulder
- Rescue Dash: outdoor natural light, slightly faster motion for jump/save, warm returns after save
- Road & Muscle: garage/market natural light, practical fluorescents allowed but warm, focus on hero prop
- Spy Agency: daytime warm, magnifier close-up, comedic not dark noir
- Payoff: return to balanced warm cream / soft wood, both families together, snack/hug

## Quality rejection triggers — photoreal cozy

Reject and rerender any shot with:
- identity drift (Clover not orange onesie, Bruno not gray 70y + burnt-orange cardigan and glasses-on-a-chain)
- unstable wardrobe or fur color shift
- deformed paws on story-critical prop (bike pump, bag, oil can, magnifier)
- impossible eyelines (Clover left polite up, Bruno right grumpy down must be preserved)
- background motion that steals focus (market crowd moving too much)
- unintended lip movement (Pip, Mimi background)
- synthetic text presented as evidence (shopping list must be post)
- dark navy tribunal lighting or cold office tone
- distress bait: animal in distress as core hook, syringe, chased, axe
- Bruno says yes first — violates Never-Yes-First locked gag
- Clover rude — violates always polite
- Max chars violated: >3 Shorts, >6 Longs
- In-house only for 3 episodes in row — must rotate to outdoor places

Every shot manifest must inherit this contract and state practical light, palette (warm cream / soft wood), wardrobe (orange onesie / burnt-orange cardigan + glasses-on-a-chain), hero prop, camera, eyeline and composition. Frame 16:9, and keep the subject clear of the left and right thirds wherever a Short will be reframed from that clip in post. Inspect beginning, middle and end frames. Log coded rejection reason. After 3 failures on same action, simplify shot or move info to controlled insert.
