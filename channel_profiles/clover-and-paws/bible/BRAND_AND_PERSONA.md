## Audience persona: The Cozy Pet Family Viewer

| Attribute | Profile |
|---|---|
| Age | Primary 22–44 family / pet lovers; secondary 45–60 (loves Bruno retired vibe) + 18–24 cat/dog Shorts |
| Interests | Cat and dog stories, anthropomorphic pets, cozy family life, cute kitten, grumpy dog, small daily dramas, kindness, wish-fulfillment |
| Pain points | Fake real animal abuse, scary AI, loud screaming pets, stories with no ending, too many characters in Shorts |
| Goal | Watch a complete cute story in 3 minutes (Short) or 9 minutes (Long), feel warm, see polite vs grumpy dynamic, share with family |
| Viewing triggers | "Clover asks Bruno...", "Bruno says no then helps", kitten polite + old dog grumpy, family chores, money/hustle (Tico), anxious mom (Mimi) |
| Return trigger | Same street (Paw Street), same faces (Clover/Bruno), new small daily problem each time, always resolved |

## Voice rules

- Write with warm plain language. Short sentences.
- Clover always polite, even after Bruno says no: "Okay, thank you Bruno..."
- Bruno always: first line NO with attitude / scoff / complaint. Second line: sigh + reluctant soft yes + helps anyway. Never says yes first attempt — locked gag.
- Mama Rosa = steady, warm, short practical lines.
- Papa Fin = calm, handy, dad-joke light.
- Pip = loyal, bright, high-energy. Usually backs Bruno before nudging him — but not on rails.
- Let characters show kindness through action, not lecture.
- End on resolved chore/help + small emotional payoff (hug, shared snack, thank you).

## Thumbnail rules — LOCKED for cat n dog

1. Use bright warm home/street set plate, not dark navy. Kitchen or porch — cozy, daylight.
2. Place 2 expressive photoreal faces on left/right occupying 40% each for Duo episodes: Clover left (polite), Bruno right (grumpy). Single family episodes: one face big.
3. Place 2-4 words max on top or bottom in warm cream with dark outline: exact line from episode, e.g., "BRUNO SAYS NO" or "CLOVER ASKS POLITELY". Must be spoken in episode.
4. Place small story-critical object in center: shopping bag, broken lawnmower, coin, etc. Must appear by 0:10 in video.
5. Use no arrows, no red circles, no shocked human faces.
6. Keep lower-right 25% x 18% clear for YouTube duration overlay.
7. Generate 3840x2160 master, keep critical faces inside central 80%, verify at 10% scale (mobile).
8. For Duo 70% episodes, thumbnail MUST show both cat and dog to signal cat n dog channel.
