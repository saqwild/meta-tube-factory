# Story Architecture and Episode Formats — Clover & Bruno Stories
**Status**: LOCKED FINAL 2026-10-29
**Channel**: Clover & Bruno Stories @cloverandbruno
**Replaces**: previous prototype tribunal story engine — ABANDONED

## Purpose of this document

This file defines how a **Clover & Bruno Stories** story is conceived, tested and shaped before scriptwriting. It governs full channel rather than prescribing plot of one episode.

Paw Street is repeatable presentation frame. Underlying story may be chore, wish, small money trouble, trip, family help, but every episode must deliver same audience promise:

> Polite Clover (always with Mama Rosa + Papa Fin) has a small wish or chore. She politely asks 70-year-old retired grumpy dog Bruno (mostly with Pip) who says No first with attitude, then sighs and helps anyway — leaving both families warmer.

Pilot appears near end as one worked example, not universal plot.

## Universal story engine — cozy polite vs grumpy

Every episode follows this causal spine:

**Small wish/chore → polite Clover request → Bruno grumpy No (attitude) → Clover polite still + parent support → small complication (prop breaks, bag heavy, money short) → Bruno softening clue (Pip nudge, Rosa kindness, Clover thank you) → Bruno reluctant soft yes + helps → shared payoff (snack, hug, thank you, chore done)**

Decision does not have to be financial. It may be fixing bike, carrying groceries, cooking, cleaning porch, saving coins, preparing trip.

### One-sentence premise formula

> When polite Clover needs **[small task]** and politely asks **[Bruno or parent]**, **[grumpy No / obstacle]** makes it harder — but **[Bruno's soft yes + help]** makes Paw Street warmer.

**Duo Clover-led example:** When polite Clover needs her bike tire fixed before school and asks grumpy 70-year-old Bruno who says "I'm retired, not your repairman," her tire stays flat — but Pip finds the pump and Bruno sighs and helps anyway.

**Duo Bruno-led example:** When grumpy Bruno wants quiet porch and polite Clover's lemonade stand is noisy, he says No to helping — but when stand falls, he helps rebuild.

**Clover Only example:** When Clover wishes to bake cheese pizza and Mama Rosa is tired, Clover helps with dishes first and wish comes true.

**Bruno Only example:** When Pip loses his bandana and Bruno says "Not my problem," he then searches garage anyway.

## Eight required plot components — cozy family

| Component | Development question | What good execution looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Wish / chore contract | What small human-like task does Clover or Bruno need? | Audience understands immediately: bike, groceries, laundry, coins, trip |
| Polite request | How does Clover ask? | Gentle, soft, "Please Bruno, could you..." — always polite, even after No |
| Grumpy No | How does Bruno say No first? | Arms crossed, scoff, "No. I'm 70, I'm retired." — locked gag, must happen within 45s |
| Immediate obstacle | What visible small problem remains? | Flat tire, heavy bag, messy kitchen, broken pump |
| Helping clue | What makes Bruno soften? | Pip nudge, Rosa warm line, Clover thank you, small kindness |
| Reluctant yes + help | How does Bruno help anyway? | Sigh, "Fine, come on," uncrosses arms, soft eyes, action fixing/carrying |
| Shared payoff | What measurable and emotional result? | Chore done + snack/hug/thank you + both families together |
| Warm lesson (not preachy) | What small kindness is shown? | Action shows kindness, not lecture: help done, not moral speech |

### Bruno gag rule — LOCKED

Bruno never says yes in one attempt. Reversal is not plot twist but character softening: grumpy outside → soft inside.

Good:
> Bruno says "No, I'm not your repairman" (arms crossed), then sighs, finds pump, helps fix bike, says "Don't tell anyone I'm soft."

Weak (reject):
> Bruno says "Yes sure!" immediately — violates core gag, episode must be rerendered.

## Character functions within a case

Finished episode normally uses 2-3 speaking characters Shorts, 3-6 Longs. These are story functions, not permanently assigned cast beyond locks.

| Function | Dramatic job | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| Wisher / requester | Clover polite kitten — wants chore done, wish | What small task does she need? |
| Grumpy helper | Bruno 70y retired dog — says No first, then helps | Why does he say No, then what makes him soft? |
| Steady parent | Mama Rosa / Papa Fin — grounds, gives practical help, warm | How do parents support Clover politely? |
| Loyal sidekick | Pip puppy — loyal to Bruno, but whose position this episode is a story choice | What does Pip actually want here, and whose side does that put him on? |
| Street complication | Mimi anxious / Tico hustling — adds small obstacle or humor | What small street trouble adds variety without distress? |

Clover usually with at least one parent; Bruno usually with Pip — defaults, departed from deliberately (CAST.md §4). Max 3 on screen Shorts, even Duo.

### Emotional ownership

Every episode must identify one emotional owner: usually Clover (polite wish) or Bruno (grumpy but wants to help). Person whose small loss or kindness gives chore meaning.

Without emotional owner, episode risks becoming generic AI slop: cat paints, cat cooks, no heart.

## Three-act episode architecture

### For Shorts 160-180s target (growth driver)

| Act | Approx. time | Story work |
|---|---|---|
| Act I — Wish + polite ask | 0:00-0:30 | Show chore/wish within 10s, Clover polite request by 0:25, establish Bruno porch/kitchen |
| Act II — No + obstacle | 0:30-1:30 | Bruno says No with attitude (0:30-0:45), Clover polite still, small complication (flat tire, heavy bag), Pip/Rosa clue |
| Act III — Soft yes + payoff | 1:30-2:40 | Bruno sigh + reluctant soft yes + helps (by 2:00), chore done, shared snack/hug/thank you |
| End screen | 2:40-3:00 | Warm thank you + Best for viewer + Subscribe, keep clear |

### For Longs 8-11 min target 9:30

| Act | Approx. time | Story work |
|---|---|---|
| Act I — Wish begins | 0:00-2:15 | Show chore/wish, polite ask, Bruno No, establish both houses |
| Act II — Cost of No + clue | 2:15-6:30 | Obstacle grows, Clover + parents try, Pip nudges Bruno, softening clue |
| Act III — Help anyway + warmer street | 6:30-9:10 | Bruno helps, chore done, emotional turn, thank you |
| End screen | 9:10-9:30 | Payoff + Best for viewer + Subscribe |

Story out = final runtime minus 20s Longs, minus 15s Shorts.

## Example episodes — LOCKED types

### Example A — Duo Clover-led 70% (Shorts target)

**Working title:** Polite Clover Asks Grumpy Bruno to Fix Her Bike - He Says No At First

- Wish: Clover bike flat before school
- Polite request: "Please Bruno, could you help? You fixed Pip's wagon before..."
- Bruno No: "No. I'm 70, retired, not your repairman." Arms crossed.
- Obstacle: No pump at home, Papa Fin at work
- Clue: Pip brings rusty pump, Clover says "Okay thank you anyway Bruno" with soft eyes
- Soft yes: Bruno sighs "Fine, come on, don't make me regret it" — fixes tire
- Payoff: Clover rides, thanks, gives Bruno cheese cracker from Mama Rosa
- Thumbnail: Clover left polite holding flat tire, Bruno right grumpy arms crossed, tire center

### Example B — Duo Bruno-led 70%

**Working title:** Grumpy 70-Year-Old Bruno Has to Deal With Polite Clover's Lemonade Stand

- Wish: Bruno wants quiet porch nap
- Polite request: Clover asks "Can we use corner of porch for lemonade stand? Please?"
- Bruno No: "No. My porch is not a market."
- Obstacle: Stand wobbles, lemonade spills
- Clue: Mimi anxious about spill, Tico tries to hustle cups, Clover cleans politely
- Soft yes: Bruno brings wooden plank to steady stand
- Payoff: Both sell lemonade, share coins

### Example C — Clover Family Only 15%

**Working title:** Clover and Mama & Papa Learn to Save Coins for Trip

- Wish: Family trip needs coins
- Obstacle: Coin jar not enough
- Help: Mama Rosa shows saving, Papa Fin fixes jar, Clover adds her coins politely
- Payoff: Trip fund complete

### Example D — Bruno & Co Only 15%

**Working title:** Retired Bruno Says No, Then Helps Anyway | Bruno & Pip Find Lost Bandana

- Wish: Pip loses bandana
- Bruno No: "Not my problem, keep track of your stuff."
- Clue: Pip sad, Bruno remembers being young
- Soft yes: Searches garage, finds bandana
- Payoff: Pip happy, Bruno soft "Don't lose it again."

## Premise selection gate — cozy

Score each proposed episode 0-2 on every dimension:

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wish/chore clarity | No identifiable small task | Task exists but vague | One specific small task (bike, bag, coins, dishes) |
| Polite vs grumpy contrast | No polite request or no grumpy No | One present | Both present, Bruno No within 45s |
| Emotional ownership | Abstract | Understandable but distant | Specific Clover wish or Bruno soft heart |
| Credible help | Help is magic or impossible | Thin | Reasonable chore help (fix, carry, cook) |
| Co-appearance | Clover solo without parents | One parent | Clover+Rosa+Fin or Bruno+Pip present |
| Prop potential | No visual object | One object | Several visual props (tire, bag, pump, coins) |
| Payoff warmth | No resolution | Adds info | Warm thank you / snack / hug, chore done |
| Production fitness | Requires many locations/crowds | Achievable with compromise | Works with 2-3 speakers Shorts, 3-6 Longs, recurring kitchen/porch/street |

**Greenlight threshold:** at least 12/16, with no zero in wish clarity, polite vs grumpy contrast, co-appearance, payoff warmth.

## Plot guardrails — cozy family

- Do not begin with moral topic and attach arbitrary characters; begin with small chore/wish
- Do not make Bruno say yes first — locked gag, always No first
- Do not make Clover rude or arrogant — always polite
- Do not make poverty, ethnicity, nationality shorthand for guilt
- Do not confuse secrecy with warmth — withheld help is useful only if Bruno helps anyway
- Do not make street characters cruel — Mimi anxious not mean, Tico hustling not stealing
- Do not rely on unsupported twist — softening must have clue (Pip nudge, thank you)
- Do not let AI be abstract villain — identify who helps
- Do not reproduce real distress rescue bait (syringe, chased) — off-putting bucket demonetized
- Do not teach actionable fraud — show kindness via action
- Do not issue preachy lesson — ending shows chore done + thank you, not lecture

## Pilot role and validation

First episode **Polite Clover Asks Grumpy Bruno to Fix Her Bike - He Says No At First** is test of flagship Duo format, not template for every later plot. It tests whether viewers:

- understand polite request vs grumpy No within 45 seconds
- remain through obstacle and soft yes (retention at 0:10 chore, 0:45 No, 2:00 yes)
- comment polite vs grumpy debate (not verdict A/B)
- recognize format when next episode changes to Clover Only or Bruno Only
- accept locked cast without confusing performer with role
- respond to story as cozy family drama rather than AI slop

Review pilot after 48 hours, 7 days, 28 days using measurements in EPISODE_SPEC.md. Do not rewrite overall story system around one upload's early performance.
