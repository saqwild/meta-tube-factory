## B. Synthetic media disclosure gate — YouTube altered content

YouTube requires creators to disclose content that is meaningfully altered or synthetically generated when it seems realistic [[1]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1).

Creators must disclose content that:
- Generates a realistic-looking scene that didn't actually occur [[2]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1)
- Makes it appear as if someone said or did something they didn't (applies to Bruno's voice)

Photoreal anthropomorphic cats and dogs could be mistaken for real if viewer misses context, so Clover & Bruno must select **Yes** under Altered Content in YouTube Studio upload flow [[3]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1) [[4]](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9kt9-L1ymFA).

What YouTube shows after disclosure:
- Label appears in video's expanded description [[5]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1)
- For sensitive topics (health, finance, news, elections) more prominent label in player [[6]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1)
- If creator doesn't disclose, YouTube may proactively apply label and penalize consistent non-disclosure with removal or YPP suspension [[7]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1)

For Clover & Bruno:
- [ ] Altered content = **Yes** selected in Studio for every upload (Short and Long)
- [ ] Description contains locked disclosure line above
- [ ] About page also says AI-generated original stories. Not real footage. Photoreal anthropomorphic cat and dog family stories — Kitten Clover and Grumpy Dog Bruno.
- [ ] Thumbnail does not mislead as real animal rescue footage

Note: Unrealistic content like "someone riding a unicorn through fantastical world" doesn't require disclosure [[8]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1), but photoreal anthropomorphic family is borderline realistic, so disclosure is required for safety. YouTube's own guidance says they may auto-apply label if content has potential to confuse [[9]](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9kt9-L1ymFA).

## D. YouTube originality and advertiser-safety gate — inauthentic content update July 15 2025

YouTube updated YPP on July 15 2025 to better identify mass-produced and repetitious content as inauthentic and ineligible for monetization [[13]](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/7/youtube-updates-its-policy-to-demonetize-inauthentic-mass-produced-ai-generated-content/) [[14]](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-youtube-ai-video-monetization-rules/).

Three buckets now redlined [[15]](https://gizmodo.com/youtube-cracks-down-on-off-putting-content-and-ai-slop-2000787956):
1. Generic, repetitive, template-based videos including channels that rely heavily on AI to produce near-identical content with little originality
2. Off-putting and emotionally manipulative (e.g., distressing animal rescue as core hook)
3. AI personas offering advice

For Clover & Bruno Stories, PASS requires:

- [ ] Not mass-produced template: each episode has new chore/objective (bike, groceries, lemonade stand) + new dialogue + Bruno's never-yes-first gag resolved differently. Same characters, new story = narrative arc, not template reuse.
- [ ] Not repetitive slideshow: each clip is a 20-second performance unit with controlled inserts, not a reused-clip compilation. Avoid 40-min compilation that reuses same footage.
- [ ] Not off-putting: no animal in distress as core, no syringe color change, no chased/attacked animals [[16]](https://indianexpress.com/article/technology/tech-news-technology/ai-generated-videos-distorting-child-youtube-feed-10590649/). Warm resolved payoff, not shock.
- [ ] Not grotesque soap opera: no buff human-bodied cat cheating, axe attack, drowning set to Billie Eilish [[17]](https://www.listenfirstmedia.com/ai-slop/) — that is flagged and removed.
- [ ] Human authorship visible: script, shot design, edit, sound, voice direction notes. YouTube rewards "thoughtful, original content" vs low-effort uploads [[18]](https://theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/07/10/youtube-rolls-out-new-rules-to-curb-ai-content-who-is-affected-and-who-is-safe.html)
- [ ] Originality over volume: YouTube's VP Trust & Safety Matt Halprin: "Same technology enables great stuff, but also enables stuff that's kind of content farming, and that's the stuff we don't want" [[19]](https://gizmodo.com/youtube-cracks-down-on-off-putting-content-and-ai-slop-2000787956)
- [ ] No AI slop signals: no voice glitches, no "two em-bee", no factual errors left as-is [[20]](https://www.howtogeek.com/im-sick-of-ai-slop-on-youtube/)
- [ ] AI use / altered content = Yes set, but disclosure alone does not limit audience or monetization eligibility [[21]](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en&authuser=1)
