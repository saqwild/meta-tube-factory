## Principle
The world must feel like a lived-in home and a quiet street without competing with dialogue. Ambient life should establish time of day, chore, and small family consequence through one or two controllable actions per shot. Cozy, not tense.

## Sound hierarchy

1. Dialogue intelligibility — Clover polite voice + Bruno grumpy old voice must be crystal clear.
2. Story-critical sound: door knock, Bruno's "No" scoff, Clover's polite "please", dish clink, lawnmower cough, shopping bag rustle, coin drop (Tico's hustle).
3. Room tone and physical ambience — warm home tone, not cold office.
4. Restrained non-diegetic score — warm acoustic guitar / soft piano only.

Native AI audio is a production layer, not final mix. Remove artifacts, replace unstable animal voice effects, normalize dialogue, maintain continuous room tone across clips.

## Music rule

Use silence or warm minimal acoustic motif under daily chores. Music may rise only at:
- Cold-open cute consequence (Clover's wish)
- Reversal (Bruno's soft yes after grumpy no)
- Final emotional payoff (help done, family hug)

Music must never be scary, tense, or financial-drama brass. Keep it cozy family.

## Bruno vocal rule — LOCKED

Bruno is 70y retired dog. Voice = old, slightly raspy, slow, grumpy, arrogant but not cruel. First line attitude: scoff + "No." Second line soft yes: sigh + "...fine, come on." Never shout. Pip voice = young puppy, loyal, bright.

Clover voice = young polite kitten, gentle, soft, slightly high but not squeaky.

## Rejection triggers

- Ambient humans/animals draw attention or mutate faces.
- Non-speaker produces lip movement or voice (especially background Pip or Mimi).
- Room tone changes abruptly at clip boundary.
- Music masks dialogue or manufactures tension/fear.
- Story-critical sound occurs before visible action (e.g., door sound before knock).
- Kitchen looks dirty/scary or porch looks threatening — must stay warm.
