## How the strongest comparables work

| Cluster | Typical method | Strength | Gap to exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial confrontation | Intense personal questioning, real guests, clips | Emotion and comments | Often visually repetitive and personality-dependent |
| Fraud documentary | Narration, archival footage, charts, named cases | Search and authority | Limited dialogue and high research/legal load |
| AI cinematic short | Spectacle, lore, philosophical premise | Novelty and shareability | Viewers complain about incoherence and generic AI texture |
| Analog incident report | Timelines, alerts, evidence fragments | Retention and serialization | Mostly horror; little financial or ethical depth |
| Courtroom/tribunal fiction | Testimony and judgment | Natural debate engine | Direct independent supply is still thin |

## Audience complaints observed

- “AI slop” reactions when character behavior, causality or visual continuity is weak.
- Unresolved plot threads without an earned payoff.
- Historical or technical inaccuracies in AI reconstructions.
- Static talking scenes without evidence changes or new visual information.
- Obvious villains that remove the need to debate.

## Defensible market gap

Create **fictional, financially plausible moral cases** with the craft standard of a short film and the repeatability of a hearing:

1. Consequence-led cold open.
2. Recurring examiner and evidence-room identity.
3. Rotating domestic, corporate and future cases.
4. Reconstruction inserts every 30–45 seconds.
5. A reversal that changes the moral frame.
6. Two explicit verdict options and a pinned-comment poll.

## Packaging gap

Do not title uploads “Moral Tribunal Episode 4.” Exact tribunal terms show weak direct search behavior. Package the recognizable event:

- **He Saved 2,000 Jobs by Lying to the Bank**
- **My Husband Hid $180,000 in Debt—Then the Bank Called Me**
- **An AI Fired 10,000 Workers. Who Is Responsible?**

The series label appears in the thumbnail corner, description and playlist—not at the front of the title.
