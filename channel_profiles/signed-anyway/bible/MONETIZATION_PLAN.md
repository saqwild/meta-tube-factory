# 90-Day Monetization Plan

## Starting state

The channel has zero videos and no audience baseline. Revenue projections are scenarios, not promises. Public YouTube pages do not reveal a creator’s RPM, sponsor fees or affiliate conversions.

## Before full YPP eligibility

| Stream | Launch action | Risk/control |
|---|---|---|
| Email list | Free **Moral Ledger Case Pack**: one printable case, evidence sheet and verdict worksheet | Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data; clear privacy notice |
| Affiliate links | Only tools actually used: editing, sound, design, books on negotiation/ethics | Disclose affiliate relationship above the fold; no finance-product pushing |
| Digital product | US$9–19 printable “Debate Night” case pack after audience demand is proven | Sell entertainment/education, not legal or financial advice |
| Support | Optional Ko-fi/Patreon after 3–4 episodes; offer scripts, alternate evidence and production diaries | Do not gate the canonical verdict; channel has no official verdict |
| Early sponsors | Approach workflow, learning, productivity or privacy brands only after repeatable views exist | No payday loans, gambling, dubious trading, credit-repair or guaranteed-return sponsors |

Expanded/full YPP thresholds and available features can change by country and date; verify in YouTube Studio immediately before planning around them.

## Post-YPP revenue stack

| Stream | Planning range | Activation condition |
|---|---:|---|
| Long-form AdSense | Model with **US$3–8 RPM** until the channel has its own data | Full YPP access and advertiser-safe uploads |
| Shorts ad share | Treat as discovery bonus, not core economics | Shorts scale and eligible YPP tier |
| Sponsorship integration | Quote from expected views × **US$20–40 sponsor CPM**, then adjust for rights, exclusivity and workload | At least 4–6 uploads with stable view floor and audience geography |
| Affiliate | No fixed forecast before link-click data | Audience asks about tools/books and disclosure process is stable |
| Membership/support | US$3–8 entry tier | Returning-viewer community and consistent monthly extras |
| Digital case packs | US$9–29 | Email list and repeated requests for cases/scripts |

RPM means creator revenue per 1,000 views after platform effects; sponsor CPM is a separate pricing framework. Neither is guaranteed.

### Scenario examples

| Monthly long-form views | AdSense at $3 RPM | AdSense at $8 RPM |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 50,000 | $150 | $400 |
| 250,000 | $750 | $2,000 |
| 1,000,000 | $3,000 | $8,000 |

For a video expected to reach 50,000 views, a $20–40 sponsor CPM implies a planning range of $1,000–$2,000 before adjustments. A new channel should not quote this until it has a defensible expected-view baseline.

## Funnel

`Long-form case → verdict CTA → free case pack → email sequence → paid debate pack / supporter membership`

Email sequence:

1. Deliver the case pack and ask for the verdict.
2. Share one omitted clue and link to the next episode.
3. Offer a paid case bundle only after demonstrating value.

## 90-day roadmap

| Period | Actions | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Publish four long videos/eight Shorts; build simple landing page; offer one free case PDF; add disclosed tool/book links | Baseline views, email opt-ins, link CTR and returning viewers established |
| Days 31–60 | Publish four more longs; survey viewers; test a $9–19 case pack; create sponsor one-sheet but do not mass-pitch | First product sales or clear rejection signal; stable per-video view range |
| Days 61–90 | Publish four more longs; refine bundle; pitch 10–15 tightly matched sponsors if median views justify it; activate eligible YPP features | At least two non-ad revenue sources tested and one repeatable |

## Sponsor acceptance gate

- Product is legal and reputation-compatible in target geographies.
- Claims are independently supportable.
- Compensation, usage rights, exclusivity and revision rounds are written.
- YouTube paid-promotion toggle is enabled.
- Verbal and written disclosure appears before the viewer must click “Show more.”
- Sponsor does not influence the fictional verdict or require deceptive integration.
