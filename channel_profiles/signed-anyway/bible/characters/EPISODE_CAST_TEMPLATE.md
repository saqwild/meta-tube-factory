## Pairing and cooldown check

- [ ] One role per performer in this episode.
- [ ] No anchor is recast.
- [ ] Lead-performer two-episode cooldown passes or owner exception is recorded.
- [ ] Intimate-pair five-episode cooldown passes or owner exception is recorded.
- [ ] No more than two familiar repertory faces appear unless this is a crossover.
- [ ] Selected pairing supports contrast rather than redundant performance energy.

## Snapshot manifest
