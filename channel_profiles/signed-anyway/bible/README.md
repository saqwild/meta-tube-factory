# Signed Anyway — Channel Profile

This directory is the launch source of truth for **Signed Anyway** (`@signedanyway`), a new cinematic YouTube channel built around fictional financial and ethical dilemmas.

## Locked positioning

| Decision | Lock |
|---|---|
| Niche | Financial & Ethics Tribunal drama |
| Audience promise | A hidden financial decision destroys trust; viewers hear both sides and decide who crossed the line. |
| Flagship format | **The Moral Ledger** — 8–11 minute dialogue-led cases, target 9:30, with evidence, reversal and audience verdict |
| Primary tagline | **They knew the cost. They signed anyway.** |
| Banner line | **Money. Power. Betrayal. You decide.** |
| Channel category | Film & Animation |
| Production core | LTX-2.5 image-to-video, from an approved frame-0 still |
| Reference and frame-0 stills | Muse Image: identity sheets, plus one frame-0 still per clip |

## Content mix

- 40% personal money betrayal: hidden debt, forged signatures, inheritance and financial infidelity.
- 40% corporate collapse, fraud and executive ethics, including reconstruction-forward **Event Specials**.
- 20% near-future financial ethics under the **Future Verdicts** label.

Crisis Reconstruction is a cross-pillar visual device, not a fourth pillar or playlist. A reconstruction-forward corporate case carries the **Corporate Reckonings — Event Special** tag.

## Non-negotiable episode rules

1. Cases are fictional composites, not disguised allegations about real people or companies.
2. Both sides must be defensible until the final reversal.
3. Package the consequence in the title; never lead with an episode number.
4. Open on the damage, reveal evidence in stages, and end with two clear verdict choices.
5. Synthetic-media disclosure is confirmed before publication.
6. Human writing, editing and shot variation must make every upload meaningfully original.
