# 30-Day Launch Plan

## Cadence

- Long-form: Sunday, 18:00 Gulf Standard Time.
- Shorts: Tuesday and Thursday, 18:00 GST.
- First month: four long-form episodes and eight Shorts.
- Build Episodes 1 and 2 before publishing Episode 1.

Use this schedule as an initial test. After four uploads, adjust publish time from YouTube Studio’s viewer-availability and first-24-hour traffic data.

Every long-form upload belongs to the master **The Moral Ledger** playlist and one relevant subseries playlist. Any quoted thumbnail line must be spoken or shown in the episode; the featured number must appear on screen by 0:10.

## Long-form outlines

### Week 1 — Corporate Reckoning / Pilot

- Promise: a lie saved payroll but destroyed the workers’ safety net later.
- Reversal: board hid a real rescue offer; auditor delayed disclosure.
- Verdict: necessity versus betrayal.
- Thumbnail: Marcus on the right with certificate and empty locker; `2,000` bottom-left in Brass; exact spoken overlay **WHO PAID?** on the left.

### Week 2 — Money Between Us

- Promise: a spouse discovers $180,000 of debt attached to her name.
- Reconstruction: loan applications, balance transfers and the phone call.
- Reversal: part of the debt paid for her father’s care after she refused to discuss selling the house—but several signatures were still forged.
- Verdict: desperate protection versus financial abuse.
- Thumbnail: wife on the right with phone, husband softened behind; `180,000` bottom-left in Brass; a small Oxblood contradiction cue; exact spoken overlay **MY NAME?** on the left.

### Week 3 — Future Verdicts

- Promise: an optimization system removes 10,000 jobs, but no human selected a name.
- Reconstruction: board objective → model scoring → automated access revocation.
- Reversal: the “neutral” objective function secretly weighted pension liability, which a human finance team approved.
- Verdict: executive accountability versus distributed system failure.
- Thumbnail: executive on the right beside a fictional worker-badge silhouette; `10,000` bottom-left in Brass; exact spoken overlay **WHO FIRED THEM?** on the left.

### Week 4 — Money Between Us / Inheritance

- Promise: a will leaves everything to one child, but the signature does not match.
- Reconstruction: two will versions, care payments and last-week visitor log.
- Reversal: the signature is unauthorized, yet a private audio note expresses the same intent.
- Verdict: legal equality versus documented personal intent.
- Thumbnail: one sibling on the right with torn will; `3` bottom-left in Brass; exact spoken overlay **NOT HER SIGNATURE** on the left.

## Shorts structure

Each Short is 25–45 seconds:

1. 0:00–0:02: consequence image and one-line accusation.
2. 0:02–0:18: one fact supporting Side A.
3. 0:18–0:30: contradiction supporting Side B.
4. Final 3–5 sec: “The full evidence is in the related video. Verdict A or B?”

Never reveal the long-form reversal, final evidence or creator verdict. Attach the full episode through YouTube's **Related Video** field; description and pinned-comment links are supporting routes, not substitutes.

## Month-one review

After Day 30, rank the four cases by click-through rate, retention at reversal, verdict-comment rate, returning viewers, subscribers per 1,000 views and cost per approved minute. Increase the winning pillar by only 10 percentage points for Month 2; do not abandon the content mix based on one outlier upload.
