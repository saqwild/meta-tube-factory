## Description

They knew the cost. They signed anyway.

Signed Anyway creates original financial dramas and moral dilemma short films about financial betrayal, financial infidelity, inheritance disputes, corporate ethics, corporate fraud and near-future AI ethics. Each dialogue-driven fictional story reconstructs the evidence behind a hidden financial decision—then asks you to decide who crossed the line.

**The Moral Ledger** — every long-form financial and ethical case.  
**Money Between Us** — marriage, family and inheritance.  
**Corporate Reckonings** — boardrooms, fraud and executive choices.  
**Future Verdicts** — AI ethics and near-future dilemmas.  
**The Evidence** — Shorts that reveal one clue from the full case.

New 8–11 minute stories; target runtime 9:30.

Fictional disclosure: This is an original fictional dramatization created using synthetic performers, voices and environments. No real person, company or event is depicted.

## Default upload settings

- Audience: not made for kids.
- Category: Film & Animation.
- License: Standard YouTube License.
- Comments: on; hold potentially inappropriate comments for review.
- AI use / altered content: select **Yes** for realistic AI-generated episodes.
- First description line: a unique one-sentence case hook.
- Final description line: the standard fictional/synthetic-media disclosure verbatim.
- Required hashtags: `#FinancialDrama #MoralDilemma`.
- End screen: reserve the final 20 seconds for a visible verdict prompt, one **Best for viewer** video and Subscribe; keep clickable elements clear of the verdict text.
- Pinned comment: reproduce the episode's **Option A / Option B** wording exactly and ask: **“Which duty matters most, and why?”**
- Shorts destination: attach the full episode through YouTube's **Related Video** field. Description and pinned-comment links support that route but never replace it.

## Verdict-comment moderation SOP

1. Pin the exact on-screen Option A / Option B wording; never imply the creator's preferred verdict.
2. Heart and reply to thoughtful arguments from both sides. Ask shallow A/B replies: **“Which piece of evidence decided it for you?”**
3. Review held comments daily during the first 48 hours, then at least weekly while the episode remains active.
4. Hold or remove actual spam, repeated promotion, slurs, threats, harassment, doxxing and impersonation.
5. Allow disagreement, blunt criticism and “the villain is obvious” opinions. Do not manipulate the visible verdict by suppressing a side.
