# Ambient Life and Sound Contract

## Principle

The world must feel occupied without competing with dialogue. Ambient life should establish consequence, class, institution and time pressure through one or two controllable actions per shot.

## Environment library

| Environment | Background life | Diegetic sound | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence room | projection fan, paper edge movement, distant corridor silhouette | ventilation, chair pressure, pen click | courtroom crowd, gavel clichés |
| Domestic finance | appliance light, unopened mail, phone charging, neighbor shadow | refrigerator hum, traffic wash, paper envelope | excessive clutter and illegible generated bills |
| Corporate office | muted staff through glass, printer light, empty desks | HVAC, elevator chime, distant keyboard | busy extras with unstable faces |
| Factory/collapse | warning beacon, stopped line, lockers, safety gate | low machinery decay, fluorescent buzz, boot steps | spectacle that obscures human loss |
| Reconstruction | timeline indicators, one operational process, controlled data motion | location-specific machinery or alert | constant alarms, random UI noise |
| Future Verdicts | familiar room plus one plausible automated system | subtle servo, interface confirmation | generic neon city, robot clutter |

## Sound hierarchy

1. Dialogue intelligibility.
2. Story-critical sound: signature pen, transfer confirmation, lock click, production line stopping.
3. Room tone and physical ambience.
4. Restrained non-diegetic score.

Native LTX-2.5 audio is a production layer, not automatically the final mix. Remove artifacts, replace unstable effects, normalize dialogue and maintain continuous room tone across generated clips.

## Music rule

Use silence or a minimal low-frequency motif under testimony. Music may rise only at the cold-open consequence, the reversal and the verdict handoff. It must never instruct the viewer which side is correct.

## Rejection triggers

- Ambient figures draw attention or mutate.
- A non-speaker produces lip movement or a voice.
- Room tone changes abruptly at every clip boundary.
- Music masks dialogue or manufactures a false heroic/villain cue.
- Story-critical sounds occur before the visible action.
