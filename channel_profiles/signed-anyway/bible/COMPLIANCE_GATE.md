# Compliance and Publication Gate

No episode is cleared for upload until every applicable gate below is marked **PASS** by a human reviewer.

## A. Fiction and identity gate

- [ ] Story is an original fictional composite.
- [ ] Names, companies, products, logos, addresses and account numbers are invented.
- [ ] No character is visually or vocally recognizable as a real person, celebrity or executive.
- [ ] Plot does not reproduce a recent real dispute closely enough to imply an accusation.
- [ ] Final description line matches the locked string below exactly.

```text
Fictional disclosure: This is an original fictional dramatization created using synthetic performers, voices and environments. No real person, company or event is depicted.
```

If a future episode uses a real case, it requires a separate factual-production standard: primary sources, date-stamped citations, legal review for contentious claims, licensed/fair-use asset analysis, and clear dramatization labels. That workflow is not approved for launch.

## B. Financial integrity gate

- [ ] Financial mechanism has been checked for internal plausibility.
- [ ] Numbers reconcile across script, evidence graphics and dialogue.
- [ ] Episode does not present individualized financial, legal or tax advice.
- [ ] Fraud mechanics are described only to the level needed for the story; the video is not an actionable evasion tutorial.
- [ ] No guaranteed-return, get-rich-quick or misleading sponsor claim appears.

## C. Story and audience-trust gate

- [ ] Both verdict options are defensible from evidence shown on screen.
- [ ] The reversal is supported by an earlier clue and is not a random twist.
- [ ] The closing does not declare an official legal verdict.
- [ ] Title and thumbnail accurately represent the actual case.
- [ ] Synthetic media is not framed as leaked, surveillance or authentic documentary footage.

## D. YouTube originality and advertiser-safety gate

- [ ] Upload includes substantial original human writing, shot design, editing and sound work.
- [ ] Reused templates do not make the episode materially repetitive.
- [ ] The episode is materially distinct from the previous five uploads in story substance, not merely names and amounts.
- [ ] Violence, self-harm, abuse and sexual content are excluded or handled non-graphically.
- [ ] Paid promotion, affiliate relationships and gifted tools are disclosed clearly.
- [ ] **AI use / altered content** is set to **Yes** for realistic AI-generated episodes; the description disclosure is also present and is not treated as a substitute for the Studio setting.

### YPP Authorship Evidence Pack

Before publication, archive an evidence pack under the episode ID. It documents human authorship and preserves material for a channel-level YPP review or appeal; it does not guarantee approval.

- [ ] Approved story brief, timestamped script drafts and human revision notes.
- [ ] Selected screen capture of a writing or structural-revision session.
- [ ] Final prompt file, shot manifest, reference manifest and model/license evidence.
- [ ] Rejection/rerender log showing human shot selection and corrective decisions.
- [ ] Voice and performance-direction notes plus audio-edit/mix records.
- [ ] Editable custom evidence-graphic source files.
- [ ] Edit project/timeline file, timeline screenshots and a 2–5 minute narrated timeline walkthrough.
- [ ] Representative raw-generation versus final-edit comparison clips.
- [ ] Final metadata, thumbnail working file and materially different A/B concepts.

Maintain a rolling five-episode originality matrix comparing financial mechanism, relationship, competing duties, reversal structure, locations, reconstruction style, dialogue rhythm, cast use, thumbnail composition and sound treatment. Similar program grammar is allowed; copied story substance is not.

Before applying to YPP, prepare a concise channel-level making-of reel using examples from several episodes. It should show how Signed Anyway writes, directs, selects, rejects, edits, mixes and packages the work—not merely display prompts or final clips.


### Decision tree

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Model licence permits worldwide commercial YouTube display | Production may publish after all other gates pass. |
| Licence limited by viewer territory | Do not rely on YouTube geo assumptions; change the model. |
| Licence ambiguous or unanswered | Keep output to private tests until it is answered in writing. |
| Licence denied | Replace the model as core and regenerate all publication assets. |
