# Script Brief

## Episode assignment

Explain one hidden causal process behind a familiar object or event in a 9–11 minute English Micro-Journey for curious teens and adults.

## Required inputs

- One pillar and familiar trigger/object.
- Approved premise and one viewer question.
- Owner-approved `story/coverage-map.json` for coverage-enabled episodes; every in-scope MUST item must be explained, and each deliberate MUST omission needs its exact on-screen disclosure.
- Claim and visualization ledgers with direct sources.
- Episode keyword research: one primary phrase and two to four supporting phrases.
- Cast snapshot for Explorer, Control and capsule.

## Required structure

1. Familiar exterior event and question.
2. Entry transition with scale/time/cutaway disclosure.
3. Baseline before the change.
4. Mechanism in distinct causal stages.
5. Comparison or correction of a likely misconception.
6. Stable result at the scope the evidence supports.
7. Return outside and connect the hidden process to the familiar result.
8. One next-question bridge.

## Dialogue

Explorer asks concise questions or names what is visible, always as a voice over the helmet radio while the visible Explorer holds still with the visor unchanged (gestures only before or after the line), and only when visible (`VISUAL_STYLE_CONTRACT.md` voice rule). Control explains one causal step at a time and states important limits. No line may claim an event the shot does not show. Native LTX-2.5 dialogue must use the approved exact wording within the channel's spoken-word band per clip (`channel.json` → `render.spoken_word_band`).

## Deliverable

Provide a scene/beat table with time, visible action, coverage IDs, claim/visualization IDs, exact dialogue, soundscape and score. Reconcile shot count × fixed 20-second source duration separately from final editorial runtime. Include keyword payoff in the opening without keyword stuffing.

## Prohibitions

No diagnosis or treatment advice, invented emergency, magical process, anthropomorphic work crew, unsupported number/timeline, literal-footage claim, copied franchise voice, or padding that repeats the same visual action.

## Story-engine inputs (from the next LTX episode)

The writer receives the confirmed `story/story-definition.md` (`STORY_ENGINE.md` §10) alongside the ledgers. The script must realise its "you" anchor, clock, master metaphor, struggle and breakthrough, objection, practical section and callback on the arc in `STORY_ENGINE.md` §3. Explanation only, never instruction (§11). Metaphors are staged as inside places the Explorer enters, never as outside scenes.
