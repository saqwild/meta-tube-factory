# Ambient Life Contract

Ambient life makes a hidden environment feel active without inventing a second process or hiding the causal beat.

## Core rule

Every background motion must be either a documented part of the environment, a physically plausible response to the main action, or a restrained presentation cue. Random particles, unexplained workers, decorative machinery and magical energy fail this contract.

## Motion hierarchy

1. **Primary causal action:** the one event the shot must communicate.
2. **Local physical response:** displaced fluid, heat shimmer, vibration, deformation, dust or bubbles supported by the scene.
3. **Background continuity:** low-amplitude motion that establishes flow or operating context.
4. **Capsule/Explorer response:** subtle observation behavior only after the process remains readable.

Ambient motion must never compete with level 1.

## Pillar rules

| Pillar | Allowed ambient life | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Identified blood components, fluid drift, restrained tissue motion, capsule hum | Free-floating sparks, air-filled caves, random microbes, distress beacons, gore |
| Materials | Heat gradient, bubbles, grain movement, crystallization front, surface tension when sourced | Magical glow, autonomous fragments, impossible phase changes |
| Manufacturing | Existing conveyor/mold/tool movement directly related to the shown step | Unidentified workers, forklifts, extra assembly operations or branded equipment |
| Technology | Documented cooling flow, ion/electron representation, actuator motion or signal pulse with disclosed convention | Generic neon data streams, electricity arcs or UI overlays presented as literal physics |

## Audio

LTX-2.5 generates native dialogue, environmental sound and music for this profile (`channel.json` → `render.narration_mode: native_in_clip`). Use one continuous environment bed, one restrained sound tied to the principal motion and sparse score. Do not add ticking unless time is part of the supported story. Avoid cinematic booms for microscopic contact, constant sub-bass, robotic voice treatment and sound effects that imply machinery or impact not shown.

## Continuity

- Carry the same base ambience across adjacent shots in one location.
- Change ambience when scale or environment changes, and make the transition audible.
- Background entities preserve direction, density and identity unless the script explains a change.
- Off-screen Control remains audio-only; do not create a body or mouth to justify a voice.

## QA

Fail ambient life only when it obscures the required event, changes the mechanism, introduces an unsupported entity, contradicts continuity or makes dialogue unintelligible. Harmless particle-count variation and nonmaterial motion differences are acceptable.
