# Script Craft Contract

## Retention through understanding

Retention comes from a chain of useful questions, not artificial danger. Every 30–60 seconds should add a new causal step, comparison, visual state or misconception correction.

## Opening

- Show the familiar trigger immediately.
- State the primary viewer question in the first 30 seconds.
- Reveal the inside-view promise by 45 seconds.
- Use the episode’s primary keyword naturally; do not recite variants.

## Causal writing

Use visible logic: **state A → interaction → state B → why it matters**. Prefer concrete verbs such as attach, deform, heat, cool, flow, compress, align and reinforce. Define technical terms at the moment they become visible.

## Explorer / Control rhythm

- Explorer: observation, question, surprise or concise synthesis. The Explorer's lines are a voice over the helmet radio, only in rows where the Explorer is visible, and ahead of Control within a clip. The Explorer holds still, watching, while the line plays. Mark any body gesture for the speech-free moment before or after the line. The face is never revealed.
- Control: cause, boundary and correction.
- Avoid exchanges that merely paraphrase each other.
- Break long explanation with a visible event or comparison.
- Leave silent observation space for native sound and image comprehension.

## Accuracy language

Say `can`, `helps`, `in this simplified view`, `laboratory studies show` or `timing varies` when evidence requires qualification. Do not use `always`, `instantly`, `perfectly` or precise universal timing without support.

## Endings

Name the achieved state accurately, distinguish it from later processes, return to the familiar object/event and bridge to a different pillar or mechanism. Do not claim full repair, completion or safety merely because the visible segment ends.

## Revision pass

1. Remove repeated facts and synonym stacks.
2. Check each claim against its source ID.
3. Check each spoken claim against the visible action.
4. Read aloud for natural English and timing.
5. Confirm every 20-second unit has one primary action and room for the line.
6. Confirm the hook, title and description can be paid off honestly.
7. For coverage-enabled episodes, confirm each in-scope MUST item has a supported beat and each scoped-out MUST item is disclosed on screen.
