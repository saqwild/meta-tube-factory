# Factual-process compliance gate

Apply at 02b after the claim and visualization ledgers exist. The output is
story/compliance.md with per-check evidence and CLEARED or BLOCKED.

1. Every central explanation has a direct, supporting source. Distinguish
   evidence, variability, uncertainty and simplification.
2. The fictional explorer/capsule is identified as a storytelling device.
   The science is not dismissed as fiction to excuse an inaccurate claim.
3. Scale, time compression, enhanced visibility and visual metaphors are
   documented, with any needed viewer clarification planned.
4. The selected process variant is identified. A factory example is not
   presented as how every manufacturer works.
5. Medical content offers explanation rather than diagnosis, treatment,
   guaranteed outcomes or product health promises. Appropriate qualified
   review is recorded where required by channel.json.
6. Biological imagery is non-graphic and proportionate to the explanation.
   Drama comes from discovery, not fabricated emergency or fear.
7. No real-person likeness, unlicensed branded mascot, copied franchise
   character or unverified brand/factory access claim is used.
8. Originality and source rights are checked; sources inform a fresh script
   and visual treatment rather than copied narration or footage.
9. The metadata plan includes the channel's synthetic-visualization
   disclosure and public sources.
10. The actual intended audience and presentation support the general-audience
    setting; reevaluate if an episode is deliberately child-directed.

A blocked central fact, unresolved review requirement or deceptive visual
claim prevents clearance. The owner gate is the existing repo mechanism;
do not invent an additional payment, publishing or medical-service workflow.

## Story-engine additions (`STORY_ENGINE.md` §11, adopted 2026-10-29)

- **Practical sections are explanation, never instruction**, in every pillar (Body: no medical advice; other pillars: no DIY, curing or construction advice).
- Body topics that invite dangerous imitation or unsupervised medical change (for example, day-by-day alcohol withdrawal, breath-holding) need a safety boundary or must be replaced.
- **No real-world insignia on any character.** That includes national flags and mission patches on the Explorer or the "you" figure (two clips of the sibling profile's EP003 shipped one).
- **No on-screen alcohol or smoking** (owner rule, 2026-10-29): never show drinking, smoking, bottles of alcohol or cigarettes. Research and narration about them are fine. When a gate is in doubt, ask "topic or depiction?" first.
