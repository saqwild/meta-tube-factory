# Science and sourcing

## Evidence before imagery

Research precedes scripting. Read primary or authoritative domain sources:
peer-reviewed original work where appropriate, scientific institutions,
technical standards, manufacturer process documentation, and clearly
attributed expert explanations. A competitor video, AI answer or attractive
animation is not sufficient factual verification. Never invent a citation.

## Research artifact at 02

In story/research-notes.md, record the question, mechanism sequence, candidate
source URLs with access dates, disputed points, relevant variability, and
what the viewer should understand. Separate supported findings from ideas
that still need verification.

## Source artifact at 02a

Use story/sources.md with two ledgers. This is `pipeline/stages/02a-sourcing.md`'s
**Branch B** for this channel — distinct from signed-anyway's non-depiction
branch (Branch A).

### Claim ledger

| Claim ID | Exact bounded claim | Direct source URL and location | What it supports | Limits/variability | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Statuses: SUPPORTED, QUALIFIED or UNRESOLVED. Do not treat a generic homepage
as evidence for a detailed mechanism. State the actual product/process
variant when manufacturers use different methods.

### Visualization ledger

| Visual ID | Planned illustration | Factual support/claim IDs | Scale/time/color/cutaway convention | Fiction or metaphor | Audience clarification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The Explorer and capsule belong in the fictional-device column. A receptor
rendered as a docking bay or ions shown as colored particles is an explanatory
representation; do not imply literal appearance or intelligent agency.

## Accuracy boundaries

- Do not turn a typical timeline into a universal timed guarantee.
- Avoid claims that a drink affects every person identically.
- Distinguish early platelet attachment, clot stabilization and later healing (when the mechanism is biological in this class).
- Do not invent factory steps simply because they look satisfying.
- Do not show machines, tissues or molecules violating the explanation.
- A time-compressed process must be identified as such.
- Fictional danger cannot imply an ordinary safe process is a medical crisis.
- Escalate uncertain, disputed or health-actionable claims to a suitably
  qualified reviewer. Basic descriptive mechanisms use source-based editorial
  checking within the existing gates; there is no blanket extra expert gate.
  Record reviewer scope; an LLM must not impersonate a credentialed reviewer.

Any central UNRESOLVED claim blocks script clearance. Narrow the explanation,
research further, or choose a more demonstrable process.

## After research

Approved claim and visual IDs flow through script, shot plan, rendered-action
review and public source notes. Later stages read these artifacts rather than
silently introducing new facts. If a render cannot show the approved claim,
revise the shot or narration through its owning stage.

## Wording follows the evidence level (from the next LTX episode)

| Evidence | Wording |
|---|---|
| Guideline or systematic review | "research shows" |
| Human randomised trial | "in a clinical trial" |
| Observational study | "people who… tend to" |
| Animal or cell study | "**in mice**, researchers found" (name the organism) |
| Contested finding | "scientists still debate…", with both sides sourced |

Every story metaphor (`STORY_ENGINE.md` §5) is logged in the visualization ledger as a metaphor.
