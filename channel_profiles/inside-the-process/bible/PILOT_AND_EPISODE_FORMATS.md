# Pilot and Episode Formats

## Active format — long-form Micro-Journey

- 16:9 landscape.
- Approximately 9–11 minutes; target about 10 minutes.
- Fixed 20-second generated source clips at 24 fps (this repo's universal clip length — 15s under the sibling LTX profile, not here).
- Nominal 30 clips for a 600-second gross timeline; add coverage only when useful edits require it.
- English native LTX-2.5 dialogue, soundscape and music.
- One primary causal action per source clip.

## Episode architecture

| Segment | Purpose | Typical share |
|---|---|---:|
| Familiar trigger | Establish the object/event and question | 5–10% |
| Entry and baseline | Move inside and show the pre-change state | 10–20% |
| Mechanism | Follow distinct causal stages | 50–65% |
| Boundary/comparison | Correct overstatement or separate nearby processes | 10–15% |
| Return and next quest | Connect to outside result and bridge | 5–10% |

Percentages guide planning, not mandatory padding.

## Shorts derivative

- 45–60 seconds, 9:16.
- Open on one striking state contrast.
- Show one mechanism step, not the whole episode compressed.
- End with a question whose answer is in the long-form video.
- Attach the related long-form upload through YouTube’s Related Video field.
- Reframe from the master; do not mix unrelated portrait generations.

## Special formats

Comparisons, multi-part systems and factory sequences are allowed only when one viewer question remains clear. Interviews, ordinary walkthroughs, list videos and software tutorials are outside the launch format.
