# Channel strategy

## Brand

Public name: **Explained From Within**. Target handle: `@explainedfromwithin` (claimed 2026-10-29). Internal pipeline slug remains `inside-the-process`.

## Promise

**How things work - and how they are made - seen from the inside.**
Tagline: **Hidden processes, made visible.**

The recurring miniature explorer is the viewer's guide into a hidden causal
process. Each episode answers one concrete question about a familiar thing.
The storytelling devices are fictional; the explanation is researched.

## Four series

| Series | Candidate question | Inside viewpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Inside Your Body | How does a tiny cut begin to seal? | Platelets at a damaged vessel |
| Inside Materials | What happens when concrete sets? | Changes between material particles |
| Inside Manufacturing | How does molten glass become a bottle? | The material during forming |
| Inside Technology | What happens when a phone battery charges? | A clearly identified conceptual interior |

These are research candidates, not pre-cleared scientific claims or scripts.
The sibling LTX profile already produced its EP001 on the first candidate
(the tiny-cut/platelet question) — this LTX profile's own first episode is
chosen independently at `01-ideation` and does not need to avoid or repeat
that choice; it is a separate research decision.

## Repeatable story

A familiar outside-world event prompts a question. The explorer enters a
hidden setting, observes an obstacle or surprising mechanism, and discovers
a causal answer. Return outside so the viewer can connect the invisible
event to an everyday result.

The explorer witnesses the process; the capsule does not cause clotting,
charge batteries, repair cells or operate a factory unless a story explicitly
labels that action as a hypothetical demonstration rather than real mechanism.

## Audience and boundaries

General-audience curious teens and adults. English explanation with restrained
radio dialogue. The channel is not designed for preschoolers. Do not use
baby voices, familiar children's franchise characters, or toy-ad packaging.

Keep the inside-process viewpoint across topics. Exclude ordinary factory
walkthroughs without a hidden process, software tutorials, disconnected
spectacle, real rescue claims and invented health cures.

## Differentiation and commercial hypotheses

The original explorer/capsule, legible process actions, recurring visual
language and accurate explanations are the proposed differentiators.
Ads, suitable sponsors and custom educational explainers are possible future
routes, not forecast income. Neither viewer demand nor profitability is
validated by attractive test clips.

Test one full pilot before scaling. Compare audience retention and comprehension
alongside production time, failed generations and cost per usable second.
