# Monetization Plan

This is a staged commercial hypothesis, not a revenue forecast. Shared across
both production profiles of this channel — the commercial plan is
channel-level, not render-engine-level.

## Priority order

1. Build four comparable long-form episodes and establish production/retention baselines.
2. Qualify for YouTube Partner Program through eligible public watch time and subscribers under then-current rules.
3. Add sponsors only when the product fits the episode and disclosure is clear.
4. Explore licensing/custom explainers after authorship and source records are mature.

## Suitable revenue paths

- YouTube advertising on original long-form educational content.
- Sponsors in education, engineering software, science learning, productivity hardware or maker tools.
- Carefully selected affiliate links for relevant books, kits or tools actually reviewed by the channel.
- Licensed educational edits or custom visualization projects with separate factual review.
- Membership or supporter tier after a repeat-viewer community exists; possible benefits include topic voting and production breakdowns.

## Exclusions

- Medical treatment products, miracle claims, gambling, high-risk financial products or sponsor language that overrides evidence.
- Hidden affiliate links or undisclosed paid placement.
- Selling generated footage as literal microscopy, simulation or proprietary factory access.
- Repetitive mass-produced uploads that weaken originality or YPP eligibility.

## Sponsor integration

Sponsor copy is visually and verbally separated from the factual process, disclosed at the required point and limited to a short completed beat. No sponsor may approve scientific conclusions or require a product to appear inside a biological or technical mechanism where it does not belong.

## Unit economics

Track per episode: reference-generation cost, LTX-2.5 render attempts, accepted clips, GPU time, editorial repair time, total labor, cost per accepted second, thumbnail cost and caption/QA time. Compare those inputs with revenue only after publication data exists. Track this profile's own economics separately from the sibling LTX profile's — they are different render stacks with different cost structures.

## Decision gates

- After this profile's own EP004: assess retention, return viewers, search terms and cost per usable minute.
- After eight uploads across the channel (both profiles combined): decide whether to increase cadence, deepen one pillar or pause weak pillars.
- Change only one major packaging or production variable at a time.
