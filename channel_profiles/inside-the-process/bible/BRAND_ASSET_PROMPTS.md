## Thumbnail brand elements

- No permanent logo larger than 5% of frame.
- Use the capsule as an optional scale cue, not the main subject.
- Titles carry the mechanism. Primary thumbnails use the four-panel causal ladder: a six-word-or-fewer transformation phrase, input/trigger, in-process state, and outcome. A three-word context line and the fixed three-word footer are allowed when legible.
- Keep Deep Expedition Navy for text panels, Explorer Orange for one route or attention cue, and preserve episode-specific process colors.
