# EP001 script - How Bread Rises: Inside the Dough

- Logline: You left a heavy ball of dough on the counter, and a
  miniature Explorer reports from inside it as yeast gas, a gluten net,
  and oven heat turn it into the loaf you slice.
- Viewer takeaway: Bread rises because living yeast fills an elastic
  gluten network with gas, and the oven expands that gas once more
  before heat sets the structure for good.
- Target viewer: a curious general viewer who has baked once, or only
  ever bought bread. Primary feeling: controlled awe.
- Core promise: travel inside the dough and watch every state change,
  from dry ingredients to sliced loaf, with each claim sourced.
- Viewer question: "Why does bread dough rise, and what actually
  happens inside it in the oven?"
- Process boundary: mixing bowl to cooled, sliced loaf. Wheat growing
  and milling are out of scope (COV08, disclosure spoken in row 1).
- Runtime estimate: 12 rows x 20 s = 240 s (4-minute demo sample).
  Spoken words per clip target the channel band 38-42
  (channel.json render.spoken_word_band). Dialogue is authored for
  native LTX-2.5 generation at 04f; no LTX prompt prose is written here.
- Confirming story-definition.md: foam-city master metaphor retained
  (gluten scaffolding, yeast inflating bubbles); the surprise beat is
  restated without a measured bubble count, per CLM-3 QUALIFIED limits.
  No unsupported fact from the draft sheet survives.

## Writer self-audit

- Expectation/payoff: the title question (why dough rises) is asked in
  row 1, mechanised in rows 2-9, and paid off in the slice in row 12.
- Story flow: one causal journey, mixing to slicing; no tangents, no
  repeated process imagery; each row is one state change.
- Comprehension: plain active English; gluten, fermentation (as yeast
  feeding/breathing), gelatinization (as starch swelling and locking),
  and oven spring are defined when they first become visible.

  are net/catch (3→5), patience/flavour (7→11), solid city (9→10).
